  • Paid on time. Daily wage the same day, monthly pay by the 7thसमय पर पैसा। दिहाड़ी उसी दिन, महीने का पैसा 7 तारीख तकMore
  • Up to 8 hours of work a dayदिन में 8 घंटे तक कामMore
  • One day off every weekहफ़्ते में एक दिन छुट्टीMore
  • Extra hours are paid doubleज़्यादा घंटों का दोगुना पैसाMore
  • Same work, same pay for women and menएक जैसा काम, औरत और आदमी को बराबर पैसाMore
  • Helmet, gloves and safety belt are freeहेलमेट, दस्ताने और सेफ़्टी बेल्ट मुफ़्तMore

Information for daily-wage, construction, domestic and factory workers in India.

This site gives general information, not legal advice. Rules and minimum wages are different in every state. Use it to know what to ask, then confirm with your district labour office or free legal aid on 15100.
